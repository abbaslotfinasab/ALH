# Writing insight entries

Create one Markdown or MDX file per reviewed article or note in src/content/insights/.

```yaml
---
title: "Article title"
summary: "A concise summary for listing and sharing."
status: draft
publishedAt: 2026-01-01
updatedAt: 2026-01-02
category: "Digital business architecture"
tags: []
readingMinutes: 5
seoTitle: "Optional SEO title"
seoDescription: "Optional search description"
canonicalPath: "/insights/article-slug/"
---
```

- The filename becomes the content slug and public URL segment.
- Use the original publication date and slug when migrating legacy material.
- Keep drafts unpublished until editorial and SEO review are complete.
- Do not fabricate dates or republish third-party material without rights.
